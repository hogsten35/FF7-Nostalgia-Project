## Guardrails

Do not make Cipher a youthful fantasy swordsman. Avoid spiky anime hair, ornamental fantasy armor, capes, giant shoulder armor, glowing magical markings, or direct visual resemblance to an existing copyrighted JRPG protagonist. His design should communicate a veteran who has used and repaired his gear for years.
