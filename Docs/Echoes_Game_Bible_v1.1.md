1\. DESIGN OVERVIEW

Core Philosophy

Echoes is a character-driven RPG about complicity, systemic collapse,
and moral compromise. It rejects the chosen-one narrative in favor of
intimate stakes: keeping four fractured allies alive while the world
collapses around them.

Key Themes:

• Complicity - You were part of the system. Now you're running from it.

• What makes someone real - Kira may not be human, but she's something.

• Impossible choices - No resolution saves everyone. Every path costs.

Target Audience

Players aged 18+ who appreciate complex narratives, morally gray
characters, and systems that reinforce story.

Game Length & Scope

30-50 hours for full completion. Three acts with distinct
geographic/thematic regions.

2\. WORLD & SETTING

Valence: The Megacity

Population: ~100 million. Built on extraction of Remnant, a biological
planetary substrate. The city exists in three layers:

|            |                                                                             |                                                 |
|------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------|
| Layer      | Description                                                                 | Character Experience                            |
| The Spires | Sky-level elite districts. Gleaming towers, filtered air, controlled zones. | Hostile. Sterile. Disconnected.                 |
| The Plates | Mid-level working class. Government, commerce, housing.                     | Familiar but poisoned. Your old home.           |
| The Depths | Underground. Industrial zones, Remnant extraction sites, slums.             | Raw. Dangerous. Where the real cost is visible. |

Remnant: The Resource

A biological substrate—the remnants of an earlier planetary ecosystem.
Living, networked, distributed-conscious. Finite and defensive when
harvested.

What it does: Powers Valence's entire civilization. Can be processed by
specific human genetics. Extraction weakens planetary systems.

The Three Conflicting Forces

|                |                                                                              |                                                                     |
|----------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Force          | Motivation                                                                   | Complications                                                       |
| Sentinel Govt  | Wants Kira dead/recaptured. She's a prototype for mass-producing harvesters. | Terrified of systemic collapse. Willing to sacrifice ethics.        |
| Eco-Resistance | Stop extraction. Some factions want total collapse.                          | Fractured. Terrorist tactics. Ignores 100M people depend on system. |
| Syndicates     | Want Kira for profit. She produces/refines Remnant.                          | Self-interest. Exploit chaos for personal gain.                     |

4\. ENEMIES & COMBAT

Common Enemy Types

SENTINEL TROOPER (Common)

HP: 20 \| Attack: 6 \| Defense: 4 \| Speed: 6

Basic infantry. Gunfire and smoke grenades. Weak individually but
dangerous in groups.

REMNANT COLLECTOR (Common)

HP: 25 \| Attack: 5 \| Magic: 8 \| Speed: 10

Bio-engineering constructs. Can drain health. Weak to physical attacks.

SURVEILLANCE DRONE (Common)

HP: 15 \| Attack: 7 \| Defense: 2 \| Speed: 12

Fast and weak. Alerts other enemies. Vulnerable to EMP (Petra's tech).

Combat Philosophy

ATB, party vs. enemies. Speed primarily controls ATB gauge fill. Each
character has unique abilities serving combat and narrative. Tactical
problem-solving—enemies have patterns, weaknesses, positioning, resource
management, and timing.

5\. BOSS ENCOUNTERS

ACT 1 BOSS: The Extraction Chamber Guardian

Location: Sentinel Black Site \| HP: 220 \| Attack: 14 \| Defense: 12 \|
Speed: 8

Security construct. Doesn't think, only follows protocols. Phase 1:
SWEEP attacks whole party. Phase 2: LOCKDOWN (50% damage reduction). Use
Kira's RESONANCE to disrupt lockdown early.

ACT 2 BOSS: Raze Korvin (Syndicate Lieutenant)

Location: Undercity Market \| HP: 300 \| Attack: 16 \| Magic: 12 \|
Speed: 11

Criminal enforcer. Wants Kira for profit. Uses INTIMIDATE to debuff
weakest member. Has 2 SYNDICATE ENFORCERS. Kill enforcers first or they
amplify Raze's power. Petra can HACK one enforcer.

ACT 3 BOSS: Director Vance (Sentinel Command)

Location: Sentinel Spire \| HP: 400 \| Attack: 18 \| Magic: 16 \|
Defense: 14 \| Speed: 10

Architect of Kira's program. Multi-phase: Phase 1 (220-400 HP): In suit,
reduced mobility but high defense. Phase 2 (0-220 HP): Out of suit,
faster but fragile. Darrow's presence changes dialogue and combat
options.

6\. OPENING HEIST SEQUENCE

Scene 1: The Job (Cipher's Apartment, Plates Level)

Petra arrives with job offer. Shows schematics of black site. High pay.
Cipher asks minimal questions. Dialogue options show attitude
(pragmatic/cautious/aggressive). No wrong choices.

Scene 2: Infiltration (Black Site Exterior)

First enemy encounter: 2 Sentinel Troopers on patrol. Can stealth or
engage. Both paths lead to same checkpoint.

Scene 3: The Descent (Facility Interior)

2-3 combat encounters or stealth options. Deeper in: biological material
on walls, soft humming. Optional exploration finds research notes, audio
logs hinting at the program.

Scene 4: The Discovery (Extraction Chamber)

Cipher finds Kira in tank, wired to machinery. Realizes this isn't a
data heist.

Dialogue Options:

• 'Who are you?' (Curiosity)

• 'This is insane. I'm getting you out.' (Conviction)

• \[Say nothing, start disconnecting her.\] (Pragmatism)

Alarms sound. Facility goes into lockdown.

Scene 5: The Guardian (Boss Fight)

Guardian activates. Kira groggy—can't fight. Cipher must protect her. If
Kira takes damage, she displays subtle supernatural resistance.

Scene 6: The Escape (Facility to Outside)

Kira now conscious but disoriented. Cipher carries her through
collapsing corridors. 2-3 enemy groups or stealth avoidance. Emergency
exit reached.

Scene 7: The Outside (First Look at Depths)

Emerge in industrial wasteland of Depths. Petra appears from shadows.
'Job's not done until she's safe. I have a place. Move.' Petra joins
party. Safe house reached. Act 1 opens.

7\. SYSTEMS DESIGN

Combat System

Active Time Battle (ATB). Up to 4 active party members. Speed primarily
determines ATB gauge fill rate. Each character has 4-6 unique
abilities + 1 limit break.

Progression System

Experience → Levels → Stat Growth + New Abilities. Characters level
independently. Equipment: Weapons, Armor, Accessories (3 slots each).
Rarity tiers: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Legendary.

Dialogue & Choice System

Branching dialogue with consequences. No fail states but choices affect
party relationships and available paths.

Relationship Tracks: Kira (Trust/Understanding/Protectiveness), Petra
(Loyalty/Skepticism/Dependence), Darrow
(Camaraderie/Conflict/Reconciliation), Maisie (Respect/Guilt/Kinship).

UI/UX

Minimal HUD during exploration. Combat UI shows party HP/MP and relevant
statuses clearly. Enemy and boss HP numbers, percentages, and health
bars are never shown. Enemy condition is communicated through animation,
posture, VFX, behavior, dialogue, and phase changes. Menu system
organized. Art style consistent with Valence aesthetic.

Difficulty Philosophy

Echoes is intentionally demanding. Regular encounters should punish
careless play, resource attrition should matter, and major bosses should
function as preparation checks. Players may need to adjust equipment,
change tactics, stock recovery items, or gain a few levels before
overcoming a wall. Light-to-moderate grinding is part of the intended
old-school rhythm, but difficulty should teach rather than rely on
unavoidable one-shots or opaque randomness.

Enemy Progression Rule

Enemies do not automatically scale to the player level. Optional
exploration, stronger equipment, ability choices, and additional levels
should create meaningful advantages.

Major Antagonist Concept: KORVOTH

Korvoth is a villain / major antagonist concept: a dark sorcerer driven
by an insatiable thirst for knowledge. His exact relationship to
Sentinel, Remnant, Kira, Raze Korvin, and Director Vance is
intentionally undefined until a later story pass. Korvoth is not the
protagonist.

8\. ART DIRECTION & TONE

Visual Aesthetic

Cyberpunk realism meets organic decay. High-tech but degraded. Tech is
functional and worn. Biology visible and unsettling.

Color Palette: The Spires (whites, grays, cool blues—clean), The Plates
(yellows, browns, faded greens—inhabited), The Depths (dark purples,
bio-luminescent greens, reds—alive but dangerous).

Character Design

Cipher: Military practicality, scarred, modular gear. Kira: Lab origin
(white/gray) transitioning to practical. Petra: Sleek, tech-integrated.
Darrow: Sentinel uniform with personal touches. Maisie: Mining worker,
industrial scars, tools as weapons.

Tone & Mood

Dark. Thoughtful. Gallows comedy from characters in crisis. Occasional
hope but never naive. World isn't fair. Characters choose meaning
anyway.
